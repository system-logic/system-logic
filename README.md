# Evgenii Zagorodskikh

Power electronics and EMC engineer. Papers and utility-model patents on conducted emission from switching converters (2015–2019); since then, industrial power and telecommunications systems at a gas production facility. Open to research and engineering work in power electronics, EMC and machine condition monitoring; relocation and remote work are possible.

Everything here is open research with the full record kept: the constraints first, then the decisions, then what broke and how it was corrected. Results come last.

## Start here

- **[motor-fault-diagnosis](https://github.com/system-logic/motor-fault-diagnosis)** — physics-first fault diagnosis of an induction motor on the public ZZU-MCC5 dataset: healthy baseline, broken rotor bar by motor current signature analysis, rotor unbalance by vibration. Negative controls run, wrong runs kept.
- **[conducted-emissions-lab](https://github.com/system-logic/conducted-emissions-lab)** — a home pre-compliance bench for conducted emissions, 150 kHz – 30 MHz, built to re-test my own published claims. LISN, limiter, electronic load, bench background, and an error log as part of the record.
- **[basement-model](https://github.com/system-logic/basement-model)** — wiring, ventilation and three-level control of a basement room: hardwired interlocks, a programmable relay, an AI that may only request. 16 drawing sheets and an [interactive 3D model](https://system-logic.github.io/basement-model/2026-10-03-design/3d-model.html).
- **[hexapod](https://github.com/system-logic/hexapod)** — a 30 cm six-legged robot whose mechanics are code: parametric FreeCAD macros, print-ready parts, STM32 motion control, Raspberry Pi 5 stereo vision with depth and YOLO tracking; a local AI on a PC sends only high-level commands. Work in progress: built and assembled, not walking yet.
- **[decision-logic-lab](https://github.com/system-logic/decision-logic-lab)** — the research log that ties the experiments together, with the [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCWOWanUk09jwCybs0VZSIqg).
